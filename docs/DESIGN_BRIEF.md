# Design Brief

## Palette
- Light: paper `#F4F1EA`, card `#FFFFFF`, ink `#141413`, muted `#6B6860`
- Dark: paper `#111110`, card `#181817`, ink `#F0EDE6`, muted `#9E9A90`
- Accents: Cyan `#009FE3`, Magenta `#E6007E`, Yellow `#F5D000`

## Typography
- Headings: Space Grotesk
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Specs & Numbers: JetBrains Mono

## Layout
- Sharp 2px ink borders, no rounded cards or competing pastel UI colors.
- Mobile (`< 768px`): Single column with expandable burger nav.
- Desktop (`>= 768px`): 12-column split (5-col sticky target swatch, 7-col mixer and scorecard ledger).
